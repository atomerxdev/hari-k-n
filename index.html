              Hi, I'm Hari Krishnan — a cybersecurity analyst and penetration
              tester based in Kerala, India, trained at RedTeam Hacker Academy.
              I'm an ethical hacker in the making: I study how systems break so I can help fix them —
              before someone with bad intentions finds them first.
            

            

              A penetration test is a simulation of a real attack: recon, enumeration, exploitation,
              privilege escalation, and reporting. The goal is never to cause damage — it's to find
              the cracks ethically, prove they're real, and show exactly how to close them:
              "here's what's broken, here's how I broke it, and here's how to fix it."
            

            

              Off the clock I'm the same person: grinding TryHackMe and HackTheBox rooms, taking apart
              gadgets I don't need, and tinkering with a home lab full of intentionally vulnerable
              machines — because in this field, learning never really stops.
            

            

              "Security isn't a destination, it's a habit — and the best way to protect something
              is to understand exactly how it can be attacked." — why I chose this path
